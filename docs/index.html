Designed for the real task

A useful review loop, not another mystery score.

CareerLens connects the job post to the evidence already in your resume, then gives you a practical way to improve the document without overstating your experience.

Read the role clearly

Separate required and preferred skills so you can see where the posting puts its emphasis.

Inspect real evidence

Matched skills point back to the resume text that triggered them—not an unexplained label.

Compare before and after

Review the same transparent ATS-style estimate before and after editing a draft.

Improve without inventing

Optional AI assistance flags unsupported requirements and leaves the final choice with you.

A simple workflow

From job post to a stronger draft.

Every stage is built to help you make an informed edit. The full application keeps the scoring local and asks before sending text to an AI provider.

01

Bring two documents

Upload or paste a resume and one target job description. Review the extracted resume text before analyzing.

02

Check the reasoning

Explore the detected skills, source evidence, apparent gaps, and score components. Correct the inputs if needed.

03

Review each change

Use the optional enhancer, edit the draft, compare estimates, and only apply wording that is true to your experience.

Built with restraint

Useful feedback should leave room for your judgment.

A score can make a review easier to scan, but it cannot tell you how a person will perform or whether an employer will interview them. CareerLens keeps those limits visible and anchors suggestions to evidence you can inspect.

  • ✓Matching shows the text that supports a detected skill.
  • ✓A missing keyword is described as a document gap, not a personal deficit.
  • ✓The full app preserves the original until you explicitly choose to use a reviewed draft.
  • ✓AI enhancement is opt-in; the static demo makes no AI call.
